Support

Free, and staying free

The BM Translator and the Math Learning App are free for anyone. No ads, no paywall, and nothing to install.

Why I'm asking

DarkWiz Lab is built and paid for by one person. There's no company behind it and no advertiser to please — which is exactly why it can stay this direct. But it still costs time and money to keep going, and the next round of work is the expensive part.

What your support goes to:

▸More phrases and offline scanning in the BM Translator ▸More worked examples and topics in the Math Learning App ▸Keeping the free apps online and free of ads ▸Keeping everything free, with no ads and no accounts, forever
