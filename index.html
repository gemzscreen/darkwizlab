How a lesson runs

Four steps, every topic.

The loop is the same whether you are dividing fractions or finding a volume of revolution, so there is nothing to relearn when the mathematics gets harder.

Pick a topic

Choose a subject, then a branch, then a topic. Each one opens with a plain definition and a note on where the idea is actually used.

Move a value

Every measurement is a control. Change a radius, an angle, a coefficient — the figure redraws the instant you let go of the slider.

Read the solution

Each formula appears three ways: the general rule, your numbers substituted into it, and the result — with the reasoning underneath, in sentences.

Get asked

Quiz yourself on one topic or a whole branch. Answers feed mastery bars and streaks, so you can see which topics still need another pass.
